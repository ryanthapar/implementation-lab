Privacy and cookies

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This site is the Implementation Lab, a real estate coaching program from Let's Farm with Monica Thapar.

The Meta Pixel

If you choose "Accept" on our cookie notice, we load the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel. It uses cookies and tells Meta which pages you view and which buttons you click, such as the register buttons. We use this to measure and improve our advertising and to show our ads to people who may be interested.

If you choose "Decline", or make no choice, the Pixel does not load and none of this information is sent to Meta from this site.

Payments

Payments are handled by Square on its own pages. We do not see or store your card details on this site.

Changing your mind

Use the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of any page to change your choice. You can also clear cookies in your browser, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

More about Meta

Meta's use of this information is described in Meta's Privacy Policy.
