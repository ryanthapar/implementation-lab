Meet your coach, Monica Thapar

One of Canada's most dominant geographical farmers, and the coach who will show you exactly how she did it.

Monica Thapar is the #1 REALTOR® in Etobicoke† and has held that title for over a decade, consistently dominating a farm of more than 60,000 homes. Through geographical farming she ranks in the top 0.1% of TRREB and has broken the $2,300,000 mark in individual annual GCI.¶ Across her career she has sold more than 1,700 homes and over $1 billion in real estate.‡

Her journey wasn't handed to her. Monica began her real estate career with $300,000 in personal debt and worked through profound personal adversity: years of IVF, eight miscarriages, and battles with anxiety and depression. That resilience is exactly what she teaches: the mindset shift that keeps agents consistent long enough for the system to start working.

Let's Farm with Monica Thapar is her seven-figure coaching company, where she helps REALTORS® across North America build predictable, scalable businesses through geographical farming. Her coaching style is real, raw and authentic. As an active REALTOR® and #1 in her farm for over a decade†, Monica doesn't just teach farming, she lives it.

In 2022, Monica was personally selected by Premier Doug Ford to sell his Etobicoke home. That's the power of geographical farming.
